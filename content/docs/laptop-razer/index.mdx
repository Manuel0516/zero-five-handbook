## Working principles

Keep the operating system replaceable and preserve the personal environment
through reviewed source, package manifests, project lockfiles, and tested backups.
Put personal Omarchy overrides in user configuration, never package-owned files.
Keep private keys, tokens, account sessions, and personal data outside Git.

The existing Windows partitions are retained. Their presence is verified;
Windows boot and recovery functionality still need a direct test. No disk or
bootloader changes are part of this inventory pass.

## Acceptance before replacing the MacBook

- Recover selected MSI material from Delta and compare it with the recorded manifests.
- Export remaining unique MacBook work and reproduce required workflows on the Razer.
- Verify development, scientific Python, LaTeX, containers, and selected editors.
- Verify Wi-Fi, audio, camera, external displays, suspend/resume and battery behavior.
- Verify NVIDIA acceleration and local inference with the selected models.
- Complete persistent DNS, LAN reservation, SSH, VPN reconnect and inherited-identity acceptance.
- Back up the finished Razer environment and perform a restore rehearsal.

Power profiles, AI storage paths, and appearance choices need to reflect the
installed system and owner preferences. Earlier performance and battery targets
were proposals, not measurements. Do not retire the MacBook until its required
workflows and unique data have passed the migration checks.
