## Retirement — 2026-10-25

The owner requested removal of the Solid State Physics computer-lab service.
The `solid-computer-lab` container was stopped and removed with Compose. Its
Traefik labels disappeared with the container, retiring the HTTPS application
route at `solid-computer-lab.zero-five.space`. The lab and experimental
Ollama-bridge images were removed. Existing certificates were not deleted.

## Preserved work

The container mounted the actual course project at
`~/physics-degree/courses/FYSC23-Solid-State-Physics/labs/computer_project`.
That directory, its modified notebooks, untracked PDFs and other coursework were
not deleted or reset. It is independent of the retired container image.

Local deployment material remains under `~/physics-degree/docker/solid-computer-lab/`.
The Compose file is renamed to `docker-compose.yml.disabled` so a normal
`docker compose up` does not recreate the service accidentally. It contains
historical deployment settings and must be reviewed before any deliberate reuse.
Do not publish local deployment credentials or remove coursework during cleanup.

## Recovery boundary

Reinstallation is a new owner-approved deployment, not an automatic restart.
Rebuild a reviewed image, configure new authentication, restore a reviewed
Compose filename, and test the course workspace and exposure policy. Do not
reuse the former password without rotating it.
