## The story behind my MSI laptop

This laptop came into my life in a rush.

My parents had told me we would be leaving our home. From then on, I would spend one week at my mum’s apartment and the next at my dad’s. I had just spent a month of my summer painting and changing my room, finally making it feel like my own. Then, almost as soon as it was finished, it was time to leave.

I couldn’t carry my desktop between two homes, so I needed a laptop.

I wanted to try something different. Linux was a constant on my mind since 2020, for that reason I chose an AMD laptop, trading storage for what I hoped would be better compatibility to create new things. That decision stayed with me: just 512 GB to hold my whole digital life. There were countless operating system changes, ambitious experiments, and increasingly creative ways of making everything fit.

### A constant through every move

This laptop has been with me through every move since then. It has seen more operating systems than I can count and carried a little of almost everything I have tried to build.

It wasn’t quite the gaming machine I had imagined. But it was good enough to give most of my crazy ideas a chance—and I gave it plenty of those.

Projects, experiments, things that worked, things that broke, and things I rebuilt simply because I wanted to understand them. Through all of it, this laptop was there.

### One more chapter

Now, another part of my life is beginning. Maybe a research group. Maybe a master’s degree. There are exciting possibilities ahead, even if I don’t yet know exactly where they will take me.

Still, it hurts to say goodbye to the laptop that has been with me since my first high school. I had imagined it eventually joining my homelab, becoming a server alongside my other old laptops.

It has given me every last bit of performance it had to offer. And although it won’t follow me through every chapter ahead, it has already been part of so many that mattered.
